Philosophy
How I work.

Systems over heroics. A scalable sales engine matters more than one brilliant quarter. I build machines that produce results consistently.

Downside first. Every deal has a risk profile and every deployment has a human impact. I protect the base before chasing the upside.

Translate, don't pitch. I sit between engineering and the customer. The best sales leaders make complex technology feel accessible - not simplified.

Buyer-led sales. The best deals happen when you give buyers the tools and information to sell internally - not when you push. I use digital sales tools to track discovery, enable champions, and compress cycle times.

Robotics is an education sale. The buyer is investing in a capability they haven't operated before. My job is to make that investment feel inevitable - and then prove it was right.
